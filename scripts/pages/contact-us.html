Contact Us

Whether you’re traveling for business, leisure, or planning a special event, our team is here to assist you every step of the way. Conveniently located in the heart of Hinjawadi’s IT hub, our group of properties – Grand Tamanna, Premier Tamanna, Bizz Tamanna, Executive Tamanna, and Shree Inn – cater to every kind of traveler. If you’re searching for a well-connected and reliable hotel near Pune, look no further.

We’re happy to help with room reservations, banquet bookings, restaurant inquiries, and event planning. Our range of venues includes rooftop lounges, banquet halls, boardrooms, and conference spaces – perfect for weddings, meetings, and private gatherings. With professional staff and modern amenities, we ensure your experience is smooth and memorable. You can contact us at 020-66935000, 020-66539888, or 020-66751900 for hotel bookings. For banquet and restaurant inquiries, feel free to call 7507772159 or 020-69525400. For general questions or support, you can also email us at info@tamannahotels.com.

You may explore more about our services and properties through our website www.tamannahotels.com. Guests looking to enjoy meals from the comfort of their home can place online orders at orders.tamannahotels.com or grand.tamannahotels.com. If you’re a business traveler or a family visiting the city, you’ll find everything you need at one of our conveniently located properties. We take pride in being a preferred hotel near Pune, offering comfort, accessibility, and friendly service to all our guests.

Get in touch with us today and experience the warmth and care of Tamanna Hotels. Whether it’s for a short visit, long stay, or an event, let us help you make the most of your time at the best hotel near Pune for all occasions.
