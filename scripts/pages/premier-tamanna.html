Premier Tamanna
Hotel

Welcome to Premier Tamanna Hotel in Hinjawadi Pune, a perfect blend of elegance, comfort, and functionality. Strategically located in the IT hub of Pune, this hotel caters to business travelers, families, and long-stay guests looking for premium hospitality and excellent service. With its stylish interiors, modern amenities, and warm ambiance, Premier Tamanna promises an experience beyond expectations.

Restaurants
and bars

Dining at Premier Tamanna is an experience in itself. The hotel houses a multi-cuisine restaurant and bar with a seating capacity of up to 100 guests. Whether you’re celebrating a birthday, organizing a casual get-together, or simply enjoying a peaceful meal, the restaurant offers a welcoming environment paired with a variety of Indian and global dishes.

Rooms for Everyone

The hotel features 48 spacious premium rooms and 3 luxurious suites, thoughtfully designed to ensure maximum comfort. Each room is equipped with a double bed, LED TV, mini fridge, 24-hour room service, and free leased-line Wi-Fi, making it an ideal choice for both short and extended stays. Our suite rooms offer theme-based décor, extra space, and added luxuries like a bathtub for a truly indulgent stay.

Board Room at 2nd Floor

The Board Room at Tamanna Hotels offers a professional, air-conditioned space ideal for corporate meetings, presentations, and team discussions. Equipped with a projector, screen, Wi-Fi, and comfortable seating for up to 15 guests, it’s perfect for focused business sessions in the heart of Hinjawadi’s corporate district.

Conference Halls

The Conference Hall at Tamanna Hotels is a spacious, fully equipped venue ideal for seminars, workshops, and corporate events. With seating for up to 100 guests, it features high-speed Wi-Fi, projectors, microphones, and customizable layouts, making it a perfect choice for professional gatherings in Hinjawadi’s dynamic business environment.
